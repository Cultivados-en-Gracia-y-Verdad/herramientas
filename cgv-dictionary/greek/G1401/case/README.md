# Case G1401 — δοῦλος

## Purpose

This case exists to determine the translation policy for the Greek lemma **δοῦλος** according to the principles of **La Biblia Fiel (LBF)**.

The objective is not to produce a dictionary definition.

The objective is to determine the most faithful translation policy supported by the biblical text.

---

## Final Authority

The final authority is the biblical text.

Translation decisions are accountable to:

1. The source text
2. Lemma
3. Morphology
4. Syntax
5. Immediate context
6. Book context

Historical translations, commentaries, and AI research may assist the investigation but never become the authority for the final decision.

---

## Investigation Principles

The investigation will proceed according to the following order:

1. Observe.
2. Ask questions.
3. Gather evidence.
4. Research.
5. Make a translation decision.
6. Approve the translation policy.
7. Generate the project output.

No conclusion should be reached before sufficient evidence has been gathered.

---

## Scope

This case concerns only the lemma **δοῦλος**.

Questions regarding phrases, constructions, or broader translation policy belong in their own cases unless they are necessary for resolving this lemma.

---

## Expected Output

When this investigation is complete, the case will produce:

* an approved translation policy;
* documented reasoning for that policy;
* unresolved questions (if any);
* `output/lemma.json`.

The case remains available for future review and may be reopened if new evidence requires reconsideration.
