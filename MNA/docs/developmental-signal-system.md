# Integrated Developmental Signal System

## 1. Foundational Clarification

The project is no longer attempting to mechanically generate final structure directly from grammar.

Instead, MNA + ROOTS+ are now oriented toward:

> observable developmental continuity.

Grammar and movement data remain foundational, but they function as the exposed movement environment in which developmental continuity operates.

## 2. Why Developmental Signals Matter

Large-scale textual flow does not appear to arise primarily from:

- person-number continuity,
- connector continuity,
- or local grammatical continuity alone.

Instead, larger continuity appears to correlate more strongly with:

- unresolved developmental pressure,
- recurring contrasts,
- repeated testing environments,
- delayed closure,
- resumed burdens,
- escalation,
- and directional continuity.

These are observable textual behaviors.

## 3. Integrated Signal Environment

Developmental signals should not be treated as isolated independent modules.

They interact.

For example:

- unresolved contrast
may combine with:
- delayed closure,
which may combine with:
- re-entry,
which may combine with:
- escalation.

Together these may expose one sustained developmental continuity.

Therefore:

The system should internally support the full developmental signal environment from the beginning.

## 4. Full Internal Signal System

The internal system should support signals such as:

- recurring unresolved contrast,
- repeated exposure formulas,
- sustained testing pressure,
- delayed closure,
- re-entry / restoration,
- continuing burden,
- escalation,
- resolution signals,
- directional shift,
- sustained relational pressure.

These are not theological labels.

They are candidate observable developmental behaviors.

## 5. Progressive Educational Exposure

Although the internal system may support all developmental signals simultaneously, students do not need to learn all signals at once.

Training should progressively expose simpler and more observable signals first.

For example:

Early training:
- unresolved contrasts,
- re-entry,
- delayed closure.

Later training:
- escalation interaction,
- overlapping developmental pressure,
- multi-signal continuity,
- large-scale directional development.

This allows the architecture to remain unified while the educational experience remains simple.

## 6. Why This Matters

If developmental signals are implemented separately and added later, several problems may occur:

- fragmented architecture,
- incompatible renderers,
- isolated signal systems,
- duplicated logic,
- weakened continuity detection,
- loss of interacting developmental behavior.

Therefore the signal system should be integrated from the beginning even if educational exposure is gradual.

## 7. Three Interacting Layers

The system now consists of three interacting layers:

### Layer 1 — Raw Data Layer

Observable discourse data:

- clauses,
- connectors,
- movement,
- restorations,
- interruptions,
- developmental markers,
- continuity spans,
- developmental signal evidence.

### Layer 2 — Exposition Layer

Visual exposition of developmental evidence:

- overlays,
- spans,
- continuity traces,
- escalation indicators,
- re-entry markers,
- closure indicators,
- developmental maps.

This layer should remain intuitive and visually readable.

### Layer 3 — Training Layer

Developmental observation training:

- observation questions,
- guided exercises,
- developmental observation habits,
- disciplined textual observation.

This layer trains students how to observe developmental continuity directly from the text.

## 8. Dynamic Interaction Between Layers

The layers do not function independently.

The training layer strongly influences what should be exposed visually.

For example:

If students are learning unresolved contrast observation, the exposition layer should emphasize:

- recurring contrast spans,
- unresolved continuity overlays,
- re-entry markers.

If students are learning delayed closure observation, the exposition layer should emphasize:

- unresolved developmental spans,
- interruption/resumption behavior,
- deferred closure patterns.

Thus:

- training influences exposition,
- exposition influences perception,
- perception influences which data becomes useful.

## 9. Critical Guardrail

The system must aggressively resist:

- abstract theorizing,
- theological synthesis,
- symbolic abstraction,
- speculative interpretation,
- academic conceptual inflation,
- terminology-heavy systems disconnected from direct textual observation.

Every developmental observation must answer:

> Can this be directly pointed to repeatedly in the text?

If not, it does not belong in the system.

## 10. Final Educational Goal

The goal is not merely to produce outlines.

The goal is to train students to:

> observe developmental continuity directly from the discourse itself.

The renderings should help students see:

- what remains unresolved,
- what resumes,
- what intensifies,
- what continues,
- what resolves,
- and when a genuinely different developmental pressure begins.
