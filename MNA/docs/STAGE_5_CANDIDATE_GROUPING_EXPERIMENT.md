# MNA Stage 5 — Candidate Grouping Experiment

## Status

Experimental.

Stages 1–3 are frozen.

Stage 4 remains the observation workspace / signal visibility layer.

Stage 5 is the first layer that proposes structural grouping.

---

# Purpose

Stage 5 proposes local grouping candidates from accumulated observable evidence.

Stage 5 does not produce final H2/H1/H0 structure.

Stage 5 stops at reviewable local continuity groups.

---

# Boundary

Stage 5 is the stopping point for mechanical grouping assertion.

Everything after Stage 5 belongs to teaching, review, summarization, or manual writing.

The project must not keep inventing new mechanical stages in an endless theoretical chase.

---

# Inputs

Stage 5 may use:

- frozen Stage 1 finite verb data,
- frozen Stage 2 predicate anchors,
- frozen Stage 3 subject/movement/connectors data,
- Stage 4 observable phenomena.

Stage 5 must not use theology, topic intuition, or external interpretive systems.

---

# What Stage 5 May Assert

Stage 5 may assert:

- candidate local groups,
- start and end ranges,
- observable evidence supporting each group,
- continuity pressure,
- boundary pressure,
- confidence level.

Stage 5 may not assert:

- final H2 titles,
- final H1 titles,
- final H0 structure,
- theological meaning,
- authorial intent beyond observable structural evidence.

---

# Grouping Principle

Structure emerges from stacked continuity pressures.

Not from one marker.

Not from one connector.

Not from one subject change.

Not from intuition.

Candidate groups are proposed where multiple observable pressures accumulate.

---

# Continuity Pressures

Examples:

- subject continuity,
- maintained movement,
- connector environment,
- connector recurrence,
- lexical recurrence,
- repeated verb lemma,
- restoration after interruption,
- sequence movement.

---

# Boundary Pressures

Examples:

- connector environment collapse,
- new connector environment,
- sustained subject-field shift,
- sustained movement shift,
- sequence completion,
- repeated evidence stops accumulating.

---

# Output

Stage 5 should produce:

- `candidate-groups.jsonl`
- `candidate-groups.md`
- `grouping-audit.json`

Each candidate group must include:

- group id,
- range,
- anchors included,
- confidence,
- continuity evidence,
- boundary evidence,
- status: REVIEW.

---

# Important Safeguard

Stage 5 is allowed to make structural assertions only as reviewable proposals.

The correct language is:

> This appears to function as a local continuity group because...

Not:

> This is the final structure.

---

# Current Objectivity Hierarchy

| Level | Objectivity Level |
|---|---|
| finite verbs | extremely objective |
