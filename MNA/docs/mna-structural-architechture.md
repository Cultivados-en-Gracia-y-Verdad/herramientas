# MNA Structural Architecture

## Purpose

This document defines the revised structural architecture for the MNA system.

The goal is:

- reproducible extraction,
- anchor-first mechanics,
- minimized interpretation,
- visible auditability,
- delayed relational inference,
- stable downstream structure.

The architecture intentionally separates:

- finite verbal extraction,
- predicate-anchor extraction,
- structural continuity,
- connector relationships,
- unit formation,
- title abstraction.

This separation exists to prevent interpretive drift from corrupting foundational extraction layers.

# Core Principle

## Structure Emerges From Predicate Anchors

The MNA system does not begin with connector theory.

The MNA system begins with:

verified finite verbal anchors

Early structure is derived from:

- predicate anchors,
- anchor continuity,
- anchor interruption,
- subject continuity,
- movement shifts.

Connector relationships are intentionally delayed until later stages.

# Revised Structural Flow

## Step 1 — Finite Verbs

### Purpose

Establish verified finite verbal extraction from MorphGNT.

### Output

- finite verbs,
- morphology,
- person/number,
- verbal accounting,
- audit verification,
- reproducible counts.

### Characteristics

This stage is:

- deterministic,
- auditable,
- source-bound,
- mechanically reproducible.

### Example

Verified for 1corintios:

- 1309 verbal tokens,
- 1027 finite verbs,
- 182 participles,
- 100 infinitives,
- 0 unresolved forms,
- PASS.

# Step 2 — Predicate Anchors

## Purpose

Convert verified finite verbs into permanent structural anchors.

### Definition

1 verified finite verb = 1 predicate anchor

### Characteristics

Predicate anchors are:

- immutable,
- reproducible,
- enumerable,
- source-bound,
- auditable.

### Important Constraint

Predicate anchors are NOT full predicates.

At this stage:

- no full predicate spans are claimed,
- no attachment theory is required,
- no connector interpretation is required,
- no semantic structure is assumed.

### Architectural Importance

Predicate anchors become the permanent coordinate system for downstream structure.

No downstream stage may:

- remove anchors,
- merge anchors,
- reinterpret anchors,
- collapse anchors.

# Step 3 — Trunk + [S] + [M]

## Purpose

Derive early structure directly from predicate-anchor continuity and discontinuity.

### Core Principle

Structural mechanics emerge from:

- anchor sequencing,
- anchor interruption,
- person/number continuity,
- subject continuity,
- movement shifts.

This stage does NOT depend on connectors.

### Output

- trunk reduction,
- [S] subject-change markers,
- [M] movement markers,
- structural segmentation,
- continuity/discontinuity flow.

### Architectural Significance

This stage is the first major structural layer.

The system derives structure from:

verified verbal mechanics

instead of:

connector interpretation

This greatly reduces interpretive instability.

### Important Insight

Movement is treated as:

anchor-structural

before it is treated as:

connector-structural

This is a major architectural shift.

# Step 4 — Connectors + Labels

## Purpose

Introduce relational mechanics only after anchor structure already exists.

### Core Principle

Connectors do not generate the primary structure.

Instead:

- anchor structure already exists,
- movement structure already exists,
- subject continuity already exists.

Connectors now serve:

- relational clarification,
- label assignment,
- structural explanation,
- continuity classification.

### Architectural Advantage

This prevents connectors from over-controlling the structure.

Connectors become:

secondary explanatory mechanics

instead of:

primary structural mechanics

This greatly improves reproducibility.

# Step 5 — Patterns + Units

## Purpose

Identify larger structural continuity across labels and movements.

### Structure Emerges From

- repeated [M] movement patterns,
- repeated labels,
- continuity flow,
- interruption flow,
- anchor continuity,
- unit repetition.

### Characteristics

Patterns are not imposed externally.

Patterns emerge from:

- previously verified structural layers,
- repeated structural behavior,
- continuity/discontinuity mechanics.

### Output

- structural patterns,
- unit boundaries,
- continuity sections,
- repeated structural flows.

# Step 6 — Titles

## Purpose

Generate final abstractions from previously verified structure.

### Core Principle

Titles are:

- downstream abstractions,
- not structural generators.

Titles must emerge from:

- verified structure,
- verified continuity,
- verified movements,
- verified units.

Titles may not impose structure backward onto the text.

# Architectural Separation

## Important Distinction

The revised MNA architecture intentionally separates:

| Layer  | Function                         |
| ------ | -------------------------------- |
| Step 1 | Finite verbal verification       |
| Step 2 | Predicate-anchor extraction      |
| Step 3 | Structural continuity mechanics  |
| Step 4 | Connector relationships + labels |
| Step 5 | Patterns + units                 |
| Step 6 | Titles                           |

This separation exists to protect lower layers from interpretive contamination.

# Major Architectural Improvement

## Previous Problem

Earlier systems often merged:

- finite verbal extraction,
- connector interpretation,
- clause theory,
- discourse assumptions,
- semantic relationships.

This caused instability and hidden interpretation.

# Revised Solution

## Stable Anchor-First Architecture

The revised MNA system instead establishes:

stable verbal anchors first

Then later layers progressively add:

- continuity,
- movement,
- relationship mechanics,
- pattern recognition,
- abstraction.

This creates:

- higher reproducibility,
- safer structure generation,
- reduced interpretive drift,
- visible uncertainty,
- auditable progression.

# Truthfulness Principle

## Required Constraint

The system must never claim more structure than has been mechanically verified.

Therefore:

- finite verbs must be verified,
- predicate anchors must be verified,
- movement claims must be visible,
- connector relationships must be explicit,
- unit claims must emerge from prior layers,
- titles must remain downstream abstractions.

No hidden structure may be silently introduced.

# Final Summary

## Core Architectural Shift

The revised MNA architecture changes the system from:

connector-first structure

to:

anchor-first structure

This means:

- structure begins with verified finite verbal anchors,
- early mechanics derive from continuity/discontinuity,
- connectors are delayed until later relational stages,
- patterns emerge from verified structure,
- titles remain final abstractions.

This creates a more stable, reproducible, and auditable linguistic extraction system.
