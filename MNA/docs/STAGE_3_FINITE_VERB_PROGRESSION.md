# Subject Assignment Rule

Stage 3 may identify subject signals only from explicit observable data.

## Subject Source Order

### 1. Explicit Lexical Subject

An explicit lexical subject may be assigned only if it agrees with the finite verb morphology.

Required agreement:

- person
- number

If agreement fails, the lexical item may not be assigned as the subject.

### 2. Finite Verb Morphology

If no agreeing explicit lexical subject is present, the subject signal is limited to:

- person
- number

No implied subject may be expanded beyond the verbal morphology.
