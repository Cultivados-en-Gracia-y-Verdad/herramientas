## Status

NOT frozen.

Stage 3 is not an absolute-fact layer.

Stage 3 must remain tightly confined to finite-verb-centered mechanics.

---

# Purpose

Stage 3 exists to observe `[S]` and `[M]` across ordered predicate anchors.

Predicate anchors already function as independent clause candidates.

Finite verbs remain the controlling center.

---

- Stage 1 finite morphology

- anchor order

- observable adjacency

No external information allowed.

# Core Confinement Boundary

Stage 3 must remain confined to observable finite-verb-centered mechanics.

- themes

---

# Subject Assignment Rule

Stage 3 may identify subject signals only from explicit observable data.

## Subject Source Order

### 1. Explicit Lexical Subject

An explicit lexical subject may be assigned only if it agrees with the finite verb morphology.

Required agreement:

- person
- number

If agreement fails, the lexical item may not be assigned as the subject.

### 2. Finite Verb Morphology

If no agreeing explicit lexical subject is present, the subject signal is limited to:

- person
- number

No implied subject may be expanded beyond the verbal morphology.
