# MNA Renewed Direction

## 1. Redefined Purpose

MNA is not a final outline generator.

MNA is a discourse-development observation system.

Its purpose is to prepare and expose the text so humans can observe developmental continuity clearly.

## 2. What Changed

The earlier working assumption was that grammatical continuity could directly produce structural units.

That proved insufficient.

Grammar, person-number, subject continuity, connectors, and movement signals are valuable, but they do not by themselves explain large-scale flow.

The renewed target is not grammatical grouping.

The renewed target is observable developmental continuity.

## 3. Core Insight

The text does not merely move grammatically.

The text moves developmentally.

Large-scale flow appears when we observe what pressure, burden, contrast, or unresolved development is still being carried forward.

## 4. Role of Existing MNA Work

The existing MNA work remains foundational.

It exposes:

- finite verbs,
- clause anchors,
- connectors,
- subject fields,
- movement fields,
- interruptions,
- restorations,
- local continuity,
- local disruption.

These are not the final structure.

They are the exposed movement environment in which developmental continuity can be observed.

## 5. Data Exposition Goal

The next layer of MNA should expose data in a way that helps humans see:

- recurring unresolved contrasts,
- repeated exposure formulas,
- sustained testing pressure,
- continuing burden statements,
- re-entry after interruption,
- delayed closure,
- escalation,
- resolution signals,
- directional shifts,
- sustained relational pressure.

These are not interpretive labels.

They are observable developmental behaviors that must be marked directly from the text.

## 6. Guardrails

MNA must not introduce:

- theology generation,
- symbolic abstraction,
- sermon themes,
- psychological reconstruction,
- historical imagination,
- speculative meaning,
- doctrinal synthesis,
- abstract theorizing not directly markable in the text.

Every developmental observation must answer:

Can this be directly pointed to in the text?

If not, it does not belong in MNA.

## 7. New Evaluation Standard

MNA output succeeds when it helps a human observe:

- what is still unresolved,
- what continues,
- what resumes,
- what intensifies,
- what closes,
- what begins to carry a different pressure.

MNA output fails when it merely produces mechanical patterns that do not help observe developmental continuity.

## 8. Practical Direction

The next scripts and renderers should not try to force final H1 or H2 units.

They should expose developmental signals clearly enough that a human can evaluate the larger flow.

The immediate work is data exposition, not final structure generation.
