# Finding 003

# Clause Openings

Status: Preliminary Findings

---

## Research Objective

Determine whether the opening of a Greek clause provides objective evidence that assists the reader in understanding how that clause relates to surrounding clauses.

This investigation does not seek to identify discourse structure.

It seeks to determine what can be objectively observed from the Greek text.

---

# Finding 1

Clause openings are significant.

While studying Titus, it became apparent that the opening of a clause often immediately signals how the reader should process what follows.

Examples observed include:

- ἵνα
- γάρ
- καί
- δέ
- διά τοῦτο

This observation prompted further investigation.

---

# Finding 2

Runge approaches connectives differently than traditional grammars.

Rather than classifying connectives primarily by English translation or logical category, he describes the communicative task each connective performs within the discourse.

This provided a better way of thinking about clause openings.

---

# Finding 3

The Reader intentionally stops before discourse analysis.

Runge seeks to explain the communicative function of connectives.

The Reader has a different objective.

Its purpose is to expose observable grammatical evidence while allowing readers to make their own observations.

Therefore The Reader will expose clause openings but will not automatically assign discourse functions.

---

# Finding 4

Clause openings remain under investigation.

The current working question is no longer:

"Is this clause dependent?"

The current question is:

"What does the opening of this clause objectively contribute to the reader's understanding of its relationship to surrounding clauses?"

No conclusions have yet been reached.

---

# Impact

This investigation produced a new observation tool.

Clause Opening

Purpose:

Display the beginning of the Greek clause through an interlinear view.

The tool exists to expose evidence rather than interpret it.

Future research will determine whether certain clause openings consistently signal objective grammatical relationships.

---

# Conclusion

Runge provided an important conceptual shift.

He encouraged thinking about clause openings as signals that influence how readers process subsequent clauses.

However, The Reader intentionally stops short of assigning discourse labels or authorial intention.

Its role remains observation rather than interpretation.

Historical Note

This investigation marked the point at which the project shifted from searching for "dependent clauses" to investigating "clause openings."

That shift arose from direct observation while using the Clause Builder rather than from theoretical study.
