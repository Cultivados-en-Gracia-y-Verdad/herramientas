# OpenText.org

## Purpose

Research the methodology used by OpenText to identify clauses and clause relationships.

---

## Why This Matters

O seeks to expose objective grammatical observations.

OpenText has already produced a complete syntactic annotation of the Greek New Testament.

Understanding their methodology may answer questions that would otherwise require years of independent research.

---

## What We Hope to Learn

- How clauses are identified.
- How clause relationships are determined.
- Which grammatical signals are considered objective.
- Where interpretation begins.

---

## Available Resources

- XML datasets
- Annotation guidelines
- Publications
- Project documentation

---

## Questions

1. How is a clause defined?

2. After clauses are identified, how are relationships established?

3. Are relationships determined entirely from grammar?

4. Which relationships are objective?

5. Which relationships require interpretation?
