# Human Linguistic Review — Translator Gate 0

Human review is required for release-quality Gate 0.

The reviewer should not repeat deterministic counting work. Review the judgment-heavy cases:

- span boundaries;
- phrase/source relationships;
- reverse-link plausibility;
- unresolved ambiguity;
- exceptions to `lbf-approved` policy.

Approval must bind to the exact checksums of spine, phrase map, reverse links, and export.

Any artifact change makes approval stale.
