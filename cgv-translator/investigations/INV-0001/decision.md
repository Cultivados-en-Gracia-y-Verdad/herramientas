### Reason

H4428 מֶלֶךְ consistently denotes a king in the examined OT
occurrence evidence. Daniel contains 52 occurrences, and the
morphological variations affect article, number, preposition,
construct relation, or possession rather than the lexical sense.

In Daniel 1:1, both occurrences are straightforward construct
usages: "rey de Judá" and "rey de Babel."

No Daniel-specific evidence requires a rendering other than rey
(or the grammatically required rey/reyes form in context).
