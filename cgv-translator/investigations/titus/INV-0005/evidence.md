# Evidence

See `evidence/occurrences.md` for the NT occurrence table and the Titus 1:7 construction note.

Summary:

1. Literal household/fiscal steward in Luke and Romans.
2. Metaphorical steward of God’s mysteries / grace in 1 Cor 4 and 1 Pet 4:10.
3. Titus 1:7: ἐπίσκοπος described *as* θεοῦ οἰκονόμος (role metaphor), not renamed.
