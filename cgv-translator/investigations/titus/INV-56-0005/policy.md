# Policy

## Lemma

οἰκονόμος (G3623)

## LBF preferred rendering

mayordomo / mayordomos

## Do not use as default

- supervisor (collides with ἐπίσκοπος)
- administrador (business register; church is household/family)

## Consultative only

RV1909 dispensador

## Titus 1:7

ὡς θεοῦ οἰκονόμον → como mayordomo de Dios
