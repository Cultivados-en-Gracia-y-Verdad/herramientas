# Evidence

Deterministic source evidence is available in:

- `evidence/source-variants.json` — structured ketiv/qere records bound to exact source and spine checksums;
- `evidence/source-variants.md` — the same six records in a human-readable table;
- `MNA/SOURCES/OSHB/morphhb/wlc/Zech.xml` — the declared OSHB/WLC source file.

The evidence files do not choose a reading, judge the Spanish, or approve the translation.
