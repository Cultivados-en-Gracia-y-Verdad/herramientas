### Reason

οἰκονόμος = household steward (οἶκος + management).
Titus 1:7: ἐπίσκοπος … ὡς θεοῦ οἰκονόμον — as God’s steward of the household.
LBF: mayordomo (church as family/household), not administrador (business register).
Reject supervisor. RV1909 dispensador consultative only.
