## Origin Text

---

## Initial Observations

### O-001

The investigation originates from Titus 1:7.

### O-002

The current BLE provisional rendering is mayordomo.

### O-003

The phrase is ὡς θεοῦ οἰκονόμον — “as God’s οἰκονόμος” (accusative), parallel to ἀνέγκλητον εἶναι of τὸν ἐπίσκοπον.

### O-004

ἐπίσκοπος (G1985) and οἰκονόμος (G3623) are different lemmas in the same verse: office title vs. household-management role metaphor.

### O-005

RV1909 at Titus 1:7 renders οἰκονόμον as dispensador.

### O-006

Spanish Strong’s G3623 gloss list includes: tesorero, administrador, curador, mayordomo; the definition also uses “supervisor” as an English-influenced sense gloss (not a Spanish LBF proposal).

### O-007

Related noun οἰκονομία (G3622) is administered “household management / stewardship / administration,” not “supervision” as such.
