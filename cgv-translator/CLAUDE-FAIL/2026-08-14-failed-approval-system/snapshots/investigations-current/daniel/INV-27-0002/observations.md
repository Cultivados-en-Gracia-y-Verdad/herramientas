## Observations from the Daniel survey (2026-08-14)

### O-003

H5921 occurs 65 times in Daniel across 46 verses, in two lemma forms: `5921 a` (53) and
`c/5921 a` (12). The variation is the prefixed conjunction, not the base lemma.

### O-004

In the approved Daniel text, only 15 of those 46 verses contain enough instances of «sobre» to
cover their H5921 tokens. 45 of the 65 tokens (69%) fall in verses where «sobre» is short or
absent. Full table in `evidence/daniel-occurrence-survey.md`.

### O-005

Other prepositions present in the shortfall verses include «en» (20 verses), «contra» (8),
«por» (6), «delante de» (2), «junto a» (1) and «acerca de» (1).

### O-006

In some verses no separate Spanish preposition corresponds to עַל at all; the relation is carried
by the verb: Daniel 1:1 «y la sitió», 2:1 «su sueño se fue de él», 11:5 «le sobrepujará», 10:8.

### O-007

The occurrences do not all express the same relation. Daniel 9:14 «lo trajo sobre nosotros» is
superposition; Daniel 8:25 «contra el príncipe de los príncipes se levantará» is hostile
direction.

### O-008

H5921 is a preposition, not a noun or verb. The lemmas already given Book Default decisions in
this book — H4428 «rey», H5174 «bronce» — are content words whose sense is carried by the word
itself.
