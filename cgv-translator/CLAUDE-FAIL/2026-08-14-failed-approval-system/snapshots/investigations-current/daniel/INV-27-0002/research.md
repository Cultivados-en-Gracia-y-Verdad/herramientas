# Research

Consulted: OSHB lemma and morphology tagging for the 65 Daniel occurrences of H5921; the current
approved Daniel phrase artifact; the BLE provisional glosses.

Method: verse-level counting of H5921 tokens against occurrences of «sobre», then inspection of
the shortfall verses for the preposition actually used. Limitation recorded as E-005.

The survey was run specifically to test whether «sobre» could be approved as a Book Default. It
could not. The decision records that finding rather than the proposal that prompted it.
