# Evidence

## E-001 — Daniel occurrence survey

`evidence/daniel-occurrence-survey.md` lists all 46 verses containing H5921 with token counts and
«sobre» counts. 65 tokens; 15 of 46 verses covered by «sobre», 31 not.

## E-002 — Distribution of alternatives

Among the 31 shortfall verses: «en» appears in 20, «contra» in 8, «por» in 6, «delante de» in 2,
«junto a» in 1, «acerca de» in 1. These are verse-level co-occurrence counts, not token-level
alignments.

## E-003 — Absorbed occurrences

In Daniel 1:1, 2:1, 10:8 and 11:5 no separate Spanish preposition corresponds to עַל; the relation
is carried by the verb («sitió», «se fue de», «sobrepujará»). A rendering rule could not be
satisfied in these verses at all.

## E-004 — Sense variation

The occurrences span at least superposition (9:14) and hostile direction (8:25). These are not
merely different grammatical realizations of one sense.

## E-005 — Method limitation

Counts are verse-level. Daniel's alignment does not yet support unit-level mapping — 275 of 357
verses have a single whole-verse reverse-link unit — so no token-to-word mapping was available.
The survey establishes that «sobre» is not general; it does not establish the exact rendering of
each individual occurrence.
