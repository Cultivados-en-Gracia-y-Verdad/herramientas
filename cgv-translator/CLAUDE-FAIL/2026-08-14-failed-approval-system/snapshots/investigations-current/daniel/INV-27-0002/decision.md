### Reason

H5921 takes no single preferred rendering in Daniel.

It is a relational preposition. Its Spanish realization is governed by the verb that takes it and
the noun it governs, not by the lemma alone. A survey of all 65 occurrences across 46 verses found
that only 15 verses are covered by «sobre»; 45 of the 65 tokens (69%) fall in verses where «sobre»
is short or absent, realized instead as «en», «contra», «por», «delante de», «junto a» or «acerca
de» — and in Daniel 1:1, 2:1, 10:8 and 11:5 carried by the verb with no separate preposition at
all.

The occurrences also span more than one relation: superposition in 9:14 («lo trajo sobre
nosotros») against hostile direction in 8:25 («contra el príncipe de los príncipes se
levantará»). Declaring «sobre» the book default would therefore misdescribe the book, and
`DECISION_SCOPE.md` reserves Book Default for the normal sense "unless local morphology, syntax,
discourse, idiom, or sense requires a different realization" — here the exception is the majority.

Confidence is High. It is high in the negative finding — that no fixed rendering applies — which
the survey establishes directly. It is not a claim about any individual occurrence.

### Scope note

Scope is Book Default in the sense that this guidance governs H5921 throughout Daniel: every
occurrence is realized from its own context. It is explicitly not a word-replacement rule, and no
harmonization pass should impose «sobre» on this lemma.

Occurrence- or Construction-scoped decisions may be added later for particular senses without
revising this one (see questions.md Q-003).
