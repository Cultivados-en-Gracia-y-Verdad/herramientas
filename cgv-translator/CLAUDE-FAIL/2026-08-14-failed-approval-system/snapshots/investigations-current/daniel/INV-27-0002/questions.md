# Questions

## Initial Questions

### Q-001

Does H5921 require an LBF decision beyond the provisional rendering?

**Answered (decision 0.1).** Yes, but the decision is that no single preferred rendering applies.
The value of recording it is to stop the question being reopened verse by verse, and to prevent a
future harmonization pass from mechanically imposing «sobre».

### Q-002

Is «sobre» the book default for H5921?

**Answered (decision 0.1).** No. The survey shows 69% of occurrences are realized otherwise. A
Book Default of «sobre» would misdescribe the book.

superposition sense (rendered «sobre») as separate Construction-scoped decisions? This decision
