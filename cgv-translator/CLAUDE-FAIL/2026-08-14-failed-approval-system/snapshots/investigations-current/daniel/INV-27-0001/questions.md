# Questions

## Initial Questions

### Q-001

Does H4428 require an LBF decision beyond the provisional BLE rendering?

**Answered (decision 0.1).** Yes — a decision was recorded, but it confirms the provisional
rendering rather than displacing it. The value of the decision is that it fixes «rey» as the book
default and records the evidence, so later work does not reopen the question verse by verse.

### Q-002

Do the morphological variants require distinct renderings?

**Answered (decision 0.1).** No. The variants affect article, number, preposition, construct
relation or possession. Those are realized by ordinary Spanish grammar («rey», «el rey», «reyes»,
«rey de …») without changing the lexical sense.
