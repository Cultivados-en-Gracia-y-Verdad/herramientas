## Observations from the lemma profile (seeded from INV-0001, 2026-08-12)

### O-003

H4428 has 2526 occurrences in the OT. The profile is stored at `evidence/occurrences.md`.

### O-004

Daniel contains 52 occurrences of H4428, distributed across 40 verses.

### O-005

The occurrences appear in 5 distinct lemma forms, all prefix variants of the same base:
`4428` (24), `d/4428` (22), `l/4428` (4), `m/4428` (1), `c/4428` (1). The variation is in article,
preposition and construct relation, not in the base lemma.

### O-006

In the current approved Daniel text, all 52 occurrences are realized as «rey» or «reyes».
No occurrence is realized by any other lexeme.

### O-007

In Daniel 1:1 both occurrences are construct usages: «rey de Judá» and «rey de Babel».

### O-008

H4430, the Aramaic counterpart, occurs 135 times in Daniel across 94 verses and is a distinct
lemma with no investigation of its own.
