# Policy

Daniel renders H4428 as «rey».

Scope: Book Default — the normal lexical sense for this lemma throughout Daniel, realized as the
grammar of each clause requires («rey», «el rey», «reyes», «rey de …»). Guidance, not a
word-replacement rule.

Applies to all 52 occurrences across 40 verses. Does not bind H4430 (Aramaic) or other books.
