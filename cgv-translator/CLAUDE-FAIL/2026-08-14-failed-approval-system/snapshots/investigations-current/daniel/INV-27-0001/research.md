# Research

Consulted: Lemma Profile v0.1 for H4428 (2526 OT occurrences, cgv-data); the OSHB lemma and
morphology tagging for the 52 Daniel occurrences; the current approved Daniel phrase artifact.

No evidence was found requiring a rendering other than «rey» anywhere in Daniel. The investigation
therefore confirms the provisional rendering rather than revising it.

Not settled here: H4430, the Aramaic counterpart used throughout Daniel 2-7. It is a separate
lemma and is recorded as an open question (Q-003), not folded into this decision.
