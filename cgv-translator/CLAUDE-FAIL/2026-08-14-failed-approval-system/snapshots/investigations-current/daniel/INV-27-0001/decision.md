### Reason

H4428 consistently denotes a king in the examined OT occurrence evidence. Daniel contains 52
occurrences, and the morphological variations affect article, number, preposition, construct
relation, or possession rather than the lexical sense.

In Daniel 1:1, both occurrences are straightforward construct usages: "rey de Judá" and
"rey de Babel."

No Daniel-specific evidence requires a rendering other than rey (or the grammatically required
rey/reyes form in context).

### Scope note

Scope is Book Default: «rey» is the normal lexical sense of H4428 throughout Daniel, realized as
the grammar of each clause requires — «rey», «el rey», «reyes», «rey de …». It is guidance, not a
word-replacement rule.

It does not extend to H4430, the Aramaic counterpart used through Daniel 2-7, which is a distinct
lemma with no decision of its own (see questions.md Q-003).
