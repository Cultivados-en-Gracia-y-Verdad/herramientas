### Reason

Metal is too broad. 
Clearly this refers to copper or bronze. 
LBF has determined to translate it bronze.

---

### Scope note

Scope is Book Default: «bronce» is the normal lexical sense of H5174 throughout Daniel, realized as
the grammar of each clause requires («de bronce», «el bronce», «al bronce»). It governs all 9
Aramaic occurrences — 2:32, 2:35, 2:39, 2:45, 4:15, 4:23, 5:4, 5:23, 7:19 — all of which now read
«bronce».

It does not extend to H5178, the Hebrew cognate at Daniel 10:6, which is a distinct lemma governed
by its own decision (INV-27-0004).
