### Reason

«Metal» is too broad for H5178, for the same reason recorded in INV-0003 for the Aramaic cognate
H5174: it generalizes a lemma that names a specific metal.

H5178 is the Hebrew cognate of H5174 and denotes the same material; the difference between them is
one of language, not of sense. Daniel 10:6 is the only Hebrew occurrence in the book, so the Book
Default decision of INV-0003 does not reach it and a separate determination is required rather
than assumed.

«Bronce» is adopted, matching the cognate. Confidence is Medium rather than High because «cobre»
is also defensible — it is the gloss carried by the OSHB token itself — and the choice between
alloy and pure metal is a judgement about the burnished-metal comparison rather than a settled
lexical fact.

Scope is Occurrence, not Book Default, because H5178 occurs only here in Daniel. A future Hebrew
occurrence in another book must evaluate its own context.
