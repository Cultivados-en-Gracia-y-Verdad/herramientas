# Research

Consulted: OSHB morphology and lemma tagging for Daniel 10:6; the stored bleText gloss («cobre»);
the RV1909 baseline retained in the phrase record; INV-0003 for the cognate decision.

«Cobre» was considered. «Bronce» was preferred for consistency with the approved rendering of the
cognate H5174 across the book, and because the burnished-metal comparison of the context is
conventionally the alloy rather than the pure metal. This is a judgement, not a settled lexical
fact, and is reflected in the recorded confidence.
