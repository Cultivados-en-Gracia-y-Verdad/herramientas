# Evidence

## E-001 — Cognate relationship

H5178 נְחֹשֶׁת (Hebrew) and H5174 נְחָשׁ (Aramaic) are cognates denoting the same metal. The
distinction between them is one of language, not of sense.

## E-002 — Single occurrence

H5178 occurs once in Daniel. INV-0003 is scoped Book Default over H5174 and therefore does not
reach this token, which is why a separate decision is required rather than assumed.

## E-003 — Context

The token stands in a sequence of material comparisons describing the figure of Daniel 10:5-6,
alongside berilo (H8658, INV-27-0003) and the comparisons to lightning and torches of fire.

## E-004 — Inherited text

The prior «metal» reproduced RV1909 for the whole verse, evidence of inheritance rather than of
independent translation from the source.
