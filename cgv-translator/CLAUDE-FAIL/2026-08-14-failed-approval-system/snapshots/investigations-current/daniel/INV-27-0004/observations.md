## Initial Observations

### O-001

H5178 occurs once in Daniel, at 10:6, in the form נְחֹ֣שֶׁת (HNcfsa).

### O-002

H5178 is the Hebrew cognate of the Aramaic H5174. The two lemmas are distinct entries and are
tagged separately in the spine.

### O-003

Daniel 1-7 preserves the Hebrew/Aramaic language boundary; the 9 H5174 occurrences fall in the
Aramaic sections, this one in the Hebrew.

### O-004

The token is followed by קָלָ֑ל (H7044), an adjective in the same phrase.

### O-005

The pre-existing LBF text of Daniel 10:6 was byte-identical to the stored rv1909Text for the verse.

### O-006

The OSHB token gloss for this occurrence is «cobre».
