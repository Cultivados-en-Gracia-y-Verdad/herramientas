# Evidence

## E-001 — Lexical range of H8658

The identification of תַּרְשִׁישׁ is disputed in the lexical tradition. Proposals include beryl,
chrysolite, topaz and yellow jasper. No proposal commands consensus.

## E-002 — Distinct from the toponym

H8658 (gem) and H8659 (Tarshish, the place) are separate lemmas. Spanish «Tarsis» renders the
toponym in common usage, so a bare transliteration at Daniel 10:6 would direct the reader to a
place rather than to a stone.

## E-003 — Book precedent

`daniel-hebrew-translation-decisions.md` records: "Daniel 11:38 — RETAIN «Mauzim»; avoid
unnecessary interpretation." That decision concerns a term with no accepted common-noun
equivalent in Spanish. H8658 is distinguishable: it is a common noun for a stone, not a name.

## E-004 — Inherited text

The prior rendering reproduced RV1909 verbatim for the whole verse, which is evidence of
inheritance rather than of independent translation from the source.
