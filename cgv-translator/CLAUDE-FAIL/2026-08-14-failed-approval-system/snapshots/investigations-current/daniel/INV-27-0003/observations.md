## Origin Clause

Daniel 10:6

---

## Initial Observations

### O-001

The source contains two elements: the preposition כְּ and the noun תַּרְשִׁישׁ (H8658, HNcmsa).
There is no noun in the clause corresponding to «piedra».

### O-002

H8658 is a distinct lemma from H8659, the toponym Tarshish, although the gem name derives
from the place name.

### O-003

H8658 occurs once in Daniel (10:6). Elsewhere in the OT it appears in Exodus 28:20 and 39:13
(the breastplate stones), Ezekiel 1:16, 10:9 and 28:13, and Song of Songs 5:14 — in every case
in a list of, or comparison to, precious stones.

### O-004

The pre-existing LBF text of Daniel 10:6 was byte-identical to the stored `rv1909Text` for the
same verse, including «piedra de Tarsis», «metal» and «ejército».

### O-005

The OSHB token gloss for this occurrence is «topacio».

### O-006

The immediate context is a sequence of material comparisons describing the figure: body, face,
eyes, arms and feet, voice.
