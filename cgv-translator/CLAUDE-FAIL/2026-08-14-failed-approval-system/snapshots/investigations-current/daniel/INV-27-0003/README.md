## Why this investigation exists

The inherited rendering «piedra de Tarsis» supplies a classifier noun, «piedra», that has no
counterpart in the Hebrew. The clause contains only the preposition כְּ and the noun תַּרְשִׁישׁ.

---

## Objective

Determine the LBF rendering of H8658 תַּרְשִׁישׁ at Daniel 10:6.
