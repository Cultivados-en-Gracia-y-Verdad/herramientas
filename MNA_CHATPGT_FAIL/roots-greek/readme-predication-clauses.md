# ROOTS — Independent Predication Dataset

ROOTS is a constrained grammatical analysis system built on top of the MNA alignment layer.

ROOTS is not:

- a commentary engine
- a theological system
- a discourse reconstruction engine
- a topology engine
- an interpretive framework

ROOTS is a structural grammatical dataset focused on:

- finite predications
- predication independence
- subject continuity
- movement continuity
- structural progression
- unit progression

The system is intentionally conservative.

Its purpose is not to maximize automation.

Its purpose is to maximize grammatical precision, auditability, and structural honesty.

---

# PURPOSE

ROOTS exists to recover and track:

- finite predications
- independent predication progression
- subject changes [S]
- movement changes [M]
- structural continuity
- structural patterns
- unit boundaries
- unit progression

using explicit grammatical evidence with minimal interpretive inference.

---

# CORE REALIZATION

A major architectural correction was discovered during development:

Greek-based does NOT automatically mean structurally certain.

Greek grammar reliably provides:

- token stream
- morphology
- finite verb detection
- non-finite verb detection
- connector existence
- token order

But Greek alone does NOT reliably provide:

- clause ownership
- discourse hierarchy
- indentation structure
- topology reconstruction
- dependency trees
- complete clause boundaries
- full discourse structure

Earlier versions of ROOTS attempted:

- connector-driven hierarchy
- topology reconstruction
- ownership deduction
- dependency recovery

These approaches proved insufficiently reliable.

ROOTS therefore no longer attempts full discourse topology reconstruction.

---

# ROOTS STRUCTURAL UNIT

The primary structural unit of ROOTS is the finite predication.

All higher structural analysis proceeds from finite predication progression.

ROOTS does NOT operate primarily on:

- verses
- sentences
- thematic summaries
- connector chains
- discourse trees

ROOTS operates on finite predications.

---

# PREDICATION

A predication is the minimal grammatical assertion made about a subject.

A finite predication minimally contains:

- a subject
- a finite verbal assertion
- predicate content

Predicate content refers to the grammatical assertion being made about the subject.

The subject may be:

- explicit
- morphology-implied
- grammatically carried forward

A predication is NOT:

- a topic
- a theological idea
- a discourse unit
- a verse
- a sentence
- a connector chain

ROOTS operates on predications,
not on thematic summaries.

---

# INDEPENDENCE

A predication is considered independent when:

- it contains a complete finite assertion
- it is not grammatically subordinated
- it does not function as dependent grammatical content within another predication

Independence must be established from grammatical evidence,
not from perceived importance,
thematic prominence,
or interpretive significance.

---

# MOVEMENT

Movement [M] refers to observable shifts in predication progression.

Movement detection must be grounded in explicit grammatical or structural evidence,
not thematic interpretation.

ROOTS does not reconstruct implied discourse movement
from semantic intuition alone.

---

# PHILOSOPHY

ROOTS prioritizes:

- precision
- auditability
- reproducibility
- constrained inference
- grammatical honesty

over:

- elegance
- full automation
- complete topology
- polished certainty
- interpretive completeness

The controlling principle of the project is:

> the engine must never pretend to know
> more than the grammar actually provides

ROOTS follows the principle:

> ugly but honest is better

Leaving structure unresolved is preferable
to promoting unsupported certainty.

---

# TRUSTED SOURCE LAYER

The trusted grammatical layer is intentionally limited to:

1. Greek token stream
2. MorphGNT morphology
3. finite verb detection
4. connector inventory
5. raw verse text

Everything above this layer is treated as:

- candidate
- proposal
- unresolved
- human-audited structure

ROOTS intentionally limits structural certainty.

---

# GRAMMATICAL EVIDENCE

ROOTS only promotes structure from explicit grammatical evidence.

Examples of acceptable evidence include:

- finite morphology
- explicit subordination markers
- grammatical agreement
- grammatical person
- grammatical number
- explicit syntactic markers

ROOTS does NOT promote structure from:

- thematic intuition
- interpretive summaries
- inferred discourse flow
- theological assumptions
- semantic reconstruction alone
- stylistic preference

---

# SPANISH LAYER

Spanish exists for presentation and alignment display only.

Spanish does NOT determine:

- grammatical structure
- finite status
- predication independence
- subject continuity
- movement continuity
- structural hierarchy

Greek grammar remains the sole structural source layer.

---

# STRUCTURAL CERTAINTY

Every structural stage must distinguish between:

- confirmed
- candidate
- unresolved

The engine must never collapse uncertainty into artificial certainty.

ROOTS intentionally exposes structural uncertainty when grammatical evidence is insufficient.

---

# STRUCTURAL PIPELINE

ROOTS currently follows this pipeline:

```text
Greek tokens
→ MorphGNT morphology
→ finite verbs
→ finite predication candidates
→ confirmed predications
→ independence testing
→ independent predication stream
→ subject continuity [S]
→ movement shifts [M]
→ structural labels
→ structural patterns
→ unit boundaries
→ unit labels
```

---

# WHAT ROOTS DOES NOT DO

ROOTS does NOT attempt:

- full discourse reconstruction
- dependency ownership trees
- exhaustive clause topology
- automatic Paso 5
- automatic Paso 6
- complete clause nesting
- semantic discourse reconstruction
- interpretive discourse analysis

These exceed trustworthy grammatical recoverability.

---

# CURRENT LIMITS

ROOTS currently does not reliably recover:

- full clause ownership
- complete discourse topology
- nested dependency trees
- exhaustive clause boundaries
- implied discourse structure

Therefore ROOTS intentionally prioritizes:

- constrained recovery
- auditable structure
- conservative promotion
- unresolved disclosure

over artificial completeness.

## grammatical preparation infrastructure

for:

- structural auditing
- subject tracking
- movement tracking
- continuity analysis
- structural progression
- unit detection

rather than:
