# ROOTS-GREEK EPISTEMOLOGY

## Purpose

ROOTS-GREEK is a constrained structural knowledge system.

Its purpose is not:

- theological interpretation,
- devotional commentary,
- doctrinal synthesis,
- semantic speculation,
- discourse reconstruction by intuition,
- or forced structural certainty.

Its purpose is:

- mechanically observable Greek-text analysis,
- explicit certainty classification,
- auditable structural proposals,
- controlled rendering,
- and disciplined evidence handling.

The system must prefer:

unknown

over:

invented certainty

# 1. Core Principle

ROOTS-GREEK does not ask:

“What does the text mean?”

ROOTS-GREEK asks:

“What can be stated with mechanical certainty?”

This distinction governs the entire architecture.

# 2. Epistemological Layers

## 2.1 OBSERVED FACT

Observed facts are directly visible from the Greek text or its morphology.

Observed facts require no interpretive inference.

Examples:

- Greek token existence
- token order
- RMAC morphology
- finite/non-finite status
- connector existence
- lexical form
- grammatical person/number
- explicit negation markers
- punctuation present in the source text

Observed facts:

- may be automated,
- may feed downstream systems directly,
- and are considered mechanically trustworthy.

Observed facts are classified as:

FACT

## 2.2 MECHANICAL DERIVATION

Mechanical derivations are produced through deterministic rules operating on observed facts.

Mechanical derivations are not automatically true structures.

Examples:

- finite-clause candidate
- clause-span proposal
- ownership candidate
- backward connector search
- cross-verse candidate detection
- possible subordinate attachment

Mechanical derivations:

- must remain auditable,
- must preserve traceability,
- must expose their originating evidence,
- and must never silently become authoritative structure.

Mechanical derivations are typically classified as:

SUGGESTION

or:

REVIEW

## 2.3 STRUCTURAL SUGGESTION

Structural suggestions are possible discourse relationships inferred from mechanical derivations.

Examples:

- hierarchy proposals
- clause ownership suggestions
- parent-child topology
- cross-verse continuity
- reduction candidates
- discourse linkage proposals

Structural suggestions are NOT confirmed structure.

Structural suggestions:

- must visibly disclose uncertainty,
- must never render as invisible authority,
- and must remain reversible.

Structural suggestions may never silently feed:

- PASO 7,
- PASO 8,
- labels,
- movement systems,
- or final manuals.

Unless explicitly promoted.

## 2.4 PROMOTED STRUCTURE

Promoted structure is structure that has passed explicit audit and promotion rules.

Promotion requires:

- traceable evidence,
- deterministic repeatability,
- contradiction testing,
- explicit rule declaration,
- and audit visibility.

No structure may become promoted through:

- intuition,
- repeated exposure,
- visual familiarity,
- or renderer assumptions.

Promotion rules must always remain explicit and inspectable.

Only promoted structure may eventually feed:

- PASO 7 reductions,
- PASO 8 trunk rendering,
- labels,
- movements,
- and finalized manual workflows.

## 2.5 UNKNOWN

The system must preserve the category:

UNKNOWN

Unknown is a valid outcome.

The system must never force:

- ownership,
- hierarchy,
- discourse continuity,
- reduction,
- or semantic relationships.

when evidence remains insufficient.

Unknown is preferable to false certainty.

# 3. Certainty Boundary

The certainty boundary is the firewall separating:

observed evidence

from:

structural interpretation

No downstream layer may bypass the certainty boundary.

All downstream rendering must preserve certainty disclosure.

The renderer must never visually imply:

- confirmed hierarchy,
- confirmed ownership,
- or confirmed topology,

unless the structure has been explicitly promoted.

# 4. Classification System

ROOTS-GREEK uses four certainty classifications.

## FACT

Directly observable.

Examples:

- finite verb detection
- RMAC morphology
- connector existence
- lexical form

FACT may feed downstream systems directly.

## SUGGESTION

Mechanically proposed but not confirmed.

Examples:

- clause spans
- ownership candidates
- structural proposals

SUGGESTION may be rendered only as provisional evidence.

## REVIEW

Requires explicit audit.

Examples:

- cross-verse candidates
- unresolved ownership
- ambiguous attachment

REVIEW may not render as authoritative structure.

## BLOCKED

Not permitted for final structural rendering.

Examples:

- provisional topology
- unconfirmed hierarchy
- unstable tree systems

BLOCKED may exist as visible evidence only.

BLOCKED structure may never silently feed final PASO systems.

# 5. Connector Policy

Connectors must be treated as observable lexical items first.

Connector existence is FACT.

Connector function is not automatically FACT.

The existence of a connector does not guarantee:

- clause ownership,
- hierarchy,
- semantic intent,
- or discourse certainty.

Connector handling must distinguish:

- lexical presence,
- possible structural behavior,
- and promoted structural function.

The system must avoid forcing connector semantics.

Especially with:

- γάρ
- δέ
- καί
- οὖν
- ἵνα
- ὅτι

whose behavior may vary structurally.

# 6. Cross-Verse Policy

Verse boundaries are not authoritative discourse boundaries.

The system may propose cross-verse relationships.

However:

- cross-verse continuity remains REVIEW until promoted,
- and may not silently become authoritative hierarchy.

Cross-verse rendering must visibly disclose uncertainty.

# 7. Rendering Policy

Renderers must preserve certainty disclosure.

Renderers may not:

- hide uncertainty,
- flatten REVIEW into FACT,
- suppress BLOCKED status,
- or visually imply confirmed hierarchy.

Indentation alone may not imply authority.

If topology is provisional, the renderer must explicitly disclose that fact.

All render systems must preserve auditability.

# 8. Structural Fossilization Prevention

ROOTS-GREEK explicitly rejects structural fossilization.

Structural fossilization occurs when:

- provisional structures,
- guesses,
- or early assumptions

slowly become treated as unquestioned truth.

The system must preserve:

- reversibility,
- auditability,
- traceability,
- and explicit uncertainty.

No structure becomes authoritative merely because:

- it appears repeatedly,
- it renders cleanly,
- or it becomes familiar.

# 9. Auditability Requirement

Every structural layer must remain auditable.

The system must always allow inspection of:

- source evidence,
- derivation rules,
- certainty classification,
- promotion rules,
- and rendering behavior.

Invisible inference is prohibited.

# 10. Interpretation Firewall

ROOTS-GREEK is not authorized to:

- perform theology,
- harmonize doctrine,
- infer authorial psychology,
- impose semantic certainty,
- or construct devotional meaning.

ROOTS-GREEK may expose:

- observable structure,
- mechanical relationships,
- lexical evidence,
- and constrained discourse proposals.

Interpretation belongs outside the system.

# 11. Architectural Priority

The system prioritizes:

correctness over automation

auditability over appearance

certainty over coverage

unknown over forced structure

mechanical evidence over intuition

# 12. Final Principle

ROOTS-GREEK must remain:

a constrained linguistic evidence system

—not:

a certainty-generating interpretation engine
