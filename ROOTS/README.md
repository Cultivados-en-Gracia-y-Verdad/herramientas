# ROOTS

**ROOTS exists to teach people how to read Scripture attentively according to its own design.**
---

## Vision

ROOTS is a methodology for observing Scripture.

Its purpose is not to explain Scripture, but to help readers see what God has revealed by learning to observe the text carefully, patiently, and according to its own design.

ROOTS seeks to remove unnecessary barriers between the reader and Scripture, allowing the biblical text to remain the primary voice throughout the learning process.

---

## Mission

> **ROOTS is not trying to make better interpreters.**
>
> **ROOTS is trying to make better observers of Scripture.**

The goal of ROOTS is not to replace the student's responsibility to think, interpret, or draw conclusions.

Its goal is to help the student become a more attentive reader.

---

## Philosophy

Scripture speaks for itself.

Therefore, ROOTS seeks to observe before concluding, to see before explaining, and to allow the structure and movement of Scripture to establish the agenda of study.

The methodology intentionally resists the temptation to answer questions before the text raises them.

---

# Foundational Principles

## 1. ROOTS observes. The student interprets.

ROOTS does not exist to interpret Scripture.

Its responsibility is to help the reader observe what God has revealed.

The responsibility of interpretation belongs to the student.

---

## 2. Every observation must be verifiable.

Every observation should be demonstrable from the biblical text itself.

The student should always be able to ask,

> "Show me."

and ROOTS should always be able to point directly back to the text.

If an observation cannot be demonstrated from Scripture, it does not belong in ROOTS.

---

## 3. The manual is a guide, not a substitute.

The authority is Scripture.

The manual exists only to help the reader observe what is already present within the text.

Its purpose is to direct attention toward Scripture, never to replace it.

---

## 4. Let Scripture determine the agenda.

ROOTS follows the questions, tensions, structure, movement, and emphasis that arise from the biblical text itself.

It does not impose an external framework upon Scripture.

Instead, it seeks to discover the framework already present within the discourse.

---

# The Goal

The goal of ROOTS is not that students become dependent upon ROOTS.

The goal of ROOTS is that they become attentive readers of Scripture who no longer need ROOTS.

A successful ROOTS student should eventually be able to open any book of the Bible and naturally ask:

* What has God revealed?
* How has this discourse been constructed?
* What do I actually observe?

If ROOTS teaches that discipline, it has fulfilled its purpose.

---

# The Method

ROOTS is being developed progressively.

Each stage is added only after its necessity has been discovered through observation rather than predetermined design.

## R — Revelation

**Question**

> What has God revealed?

This stage gathers the textual evidence.

It remains completely within the text, observing its language, grammar, clauses, vocabulary, and textual relationships.

## O — Overview

**Question**

> How has this revelation been arranged?

This stage steps back from the individual clauses to observe the architecture of the discourse.

Current areas of observation include:

* Structure
* Flow
* Pressure
* Telos

The purpose is not interpretation, but observation of the text as a complete discourse.

The remaining stages of ROOTS will be developed only as their necessity becomes evident through continued study.

---
